# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary end users are Vietnamese customers seeking spa treatments (skincare, body, relaxation) and booking appointments.

Buyers of the product are spa/beauty studio owners in Vietnam who purchase or adopt this site as a reusable template for their business.

## Product Purpose

A website template for a real spa/beauty studio business in Vietnam, sold to spa owners. It presents the studio’s offerings and lets customers book appointments. Success means owners can customize brand, services, prices, contact details, and location, while customers can discover treatments and complete a simple booking flow in Vietnamese.

## Positioning

Premium, minimalist spa focused on skincare, body treatments, relaxation, and personalized experiences. Differentiator: a refined, calming customer experience with a premium look and a simple booking flow—not a generic spa brochure site.

## Operating Context

Customers use the site to browse rituals/treatments, learn about the studio, and request or book an appointment. Owners configure brand, services, pricing (VND), contact, and location for their studio. Local booking expectations in Vietnam matter (language, contact patterns, how appointments are requested).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Brand name for this template instance: **mori** (kept for now; owners customize brand when adopting).
- Primary CTA: book an appointment.
- Primary language: Vietnamese; content and UX target Vietnamese customers.
- Template must stay reusable: brand, services, prices, contact details, and location are owner-configurable.
- Address, hours, and email in the current build are demo placeholders — use Vietnamese-friendly examples or mark as configurable; do not treat as a real Brooklyn studio.
- Treatment menu and prices are demo — use realistic Vietnamese spa services and VND pricing.
- Therapist names are demo placeholders.
- Guest testimonials are demo content — never present as verified real reviews.
- Photos in `/public/spa-*.png` may be kept if visually suitable, or replaced when better assets are available.
- Incumbent stack: Next.js (App Router), React, Tailwind CSS, shadcn/ui components.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: mori (current template identity).
- Tone: premium, minimalist, calming; skin, body, and personalized ritual experiences.
- Visual world is not fixed here; product truth above constrains future design.

## Evidence on Hand

- Marketing page and booking modal UI in `app/page.tsx`.
- Spa photography assets: `/public/spa-hero.png`, `/public/spa-detail.png`, `/public/spa-therapist.png`, `/public/spa-treatment.png`.
- No verified customer reviews, real studio address, or production booking backend committed.
- Do not fabricate testimonials, licenses, press, or real customer claims.

## Product Principles

1. Vietnamese-first: language, services, pricing (VND), and booking cues match local customers and spa owners.
2. Booking is the job: every surface supports discovering treatments and completing a simple appointment request.
3. Premium calm, not clutter: the experience feels refined and restorative.
4. Template over one-off: brand, menu, prices, contact, and location stay easy for each spa owner to replace.
5. Honest demo content: placeholders stay clearly replaceable; never sell fictional reviews or locations as fact.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Primary audience is Vietnamese-speaking spa customers. Interface and content must be usable in Vietnamese as the primary language. No additional formal accessibility standard was set; follow sensible web defaults (keyboard, contrast, semantics) unless a standard is added later.
